Kidsdee Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Kidsdee respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Kidsdee collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Kidsdee mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

When you use Kidsdee, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • User account ID
  • Precise location information when you use nearby services

3. How We Use Information

We use this information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Allow you to sign in to the application
  • Provide clinic, school, activity, and service information
  • Show nearby locations and services
  • Improve the functionality and security of the application

4. Location Information

Kidsdee may request your device location to provide nearby clinic, school, activity, and service information. You can control location permissions through your device settings.

5. Sharing of Information

Kidsdee does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate the application and provide its services.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's services and fulfill applicable legal requirements.

8. Your Choices

You may manage certain permissions, including location access, through your device settings. You may also contact us regarding your personal information.

9. Children's Privacy

Kidsdee is designed for parents and caregivers. The application is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children without appropriate parental involvement.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

11. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact the Kidsdee team.
